7.1 From long tables to a four-way array

The tensor \(\mathcal{M} \in \mathbb{R}^{S \times A \times C \times T}\) holds \(\operatorname{logit}(q_x)\) for every sex \(s\) (female, male), single-year age \(a\) (0–109), population \(c\) and calendar year \(t\). The year axis runs over every calendar year in which at least one population has data. Before the logit, \(q_x\) is clipped to \([q_{\min}, 1-q_{\min}]\) with \(q_{\min} = 10^{-8}\) so that every value is finite. After pooling, the floor is needed only in exceptional years of very small populations (which are not part of the baseline); no observed baseline cell is at the floor.

Not every population has data in every year. Three \(C \times T\) masks record what each cell holds:

  • present — a female and a male life table exist;
  • observed — present and not exceptional: the paper’s observed mask \(O\) (§2.5), the cells the decomposition is fitted to;
  • exceptional — present and exceptional: the set \(\mathcal{E}\) used by the disruption model.

Cells that are not observed are left empty (NaN) here. How the decomposition treats them — it fills them iteratively with its own reconstruction — is the subject of chapter 04.

7.5 Where are the empty cells?

Of the \(C \times T\) country-year cells, most are empty simply because a population’s series had not started. The table accounts for every unobserved cell.

Table: classification of the unobserved country-year cells 
excl = cur["exclusions"]
excl = excl[excl["pop"].isin(tensor["pops"])]
excluded_keys = set(zip(excl["pop"], excl["year"]))
cats = {"Outside the population's HMD series": 0,
        "Exceptional (masked for the disruption model)": 0,
        "Removed in curation": 0,
        "Gap inside the HMD series (not published)": 0}
for i, pop in enumerate(tensor["pops"]):
    hmd = lt_raw.loc[lt_raw["pop"] == pop, "year"]
    first, last = hmd.min(), hmd.max()
    for j, y in enumerate(years):
        if observed[i, j]:
            continue
        if exceptional[i, j]:
            cats["Exceptional (masked for the disruption model)"] += 1
        elif (pop, y) in excluded_keys:
            cats["Removed in curation"] += 1
        elif y < first or y > last:
            cats["Outside the population's HMD series"] += 1
        else:
            cats["Gap inside the HMD series (not published)"] += 1
audit = pd.DataFrame({"Category": list(cats), "Country-years": list(cats.values())})
audit["Share (\\%)"] = 100 * audit["Country-years"] / audit["Country-years"].sum()
total = int(audit["Country-years"].sum())
assert total == int((~observed).sum()), "every unobserved cell must be classified exactly once"
latex.save_table(audit, "tbl-s3-missing-year-audit",
                 f"Classification of the {latex.fmt_int(total)} unobserved country-year cells "
                 f"($O_{{c,t}} = 0$) of the tensor.",
                 formats={"Share (\\%)": "{:.1f}"}, column_format="lrr")
latex.report("dataNUnobserved", total, desc="Unobserved country-year cells")

7.6 The change ledger

Every change made to the published HMD data on the way to the tensor is listed in one file, results/ledger/data-changes.csv, with one row per change: the step, the rule, the population, sex and years affected, and the details. It is generated from the records the pipeline keeps as it works — the curation exclusions (chapter d3), the event labels (d4), the pooling blocks (d5) and the floor applied here — so it is complete by construction.

```{python}
print("Largest differences:\n", diff.abs().sort_values(ascending=False).head(5).round(3).to_string())
latex.report("dataEzeroDiffMedian", float(diff.median()), digits=3,
             desc="Median of the article's e0 minus the HMD's own, curated life tables (years)")
latex.report("dataEzeroDiffLarge", int((diff.abs() > 0.01).sum()),
             desc="Curated life tables whose e0 differs from the HMD's own by more than 0.01 years")
latex.report("dataEzeroDiffMax", float(diff.abs().max()), digits=2,
             desc="Largest absolute difference between the article's e0 and the HMD's own (years)")
latex.report("dataEzeroDiffMedianAbs", float(diff.abs().median()), digits=3,
             desc="Median absolute difference between the article's e0 and the HMD's own, curated life tables (years)")
# The large differences have two sources: the conventions (a fixed a0, no years lived beyond 110), which
# put the article's e0 a little above the HMD's in some tables, and survival to 110 that the life table
# does not follow further, which puts it below where that survival is high.
l110 = pd.Series(np.prod(1 - qx.to_numpy(), axis=1), index=qx.index)
large = diff[diff.abs() > 0.01]
for sign, tag in ((1, "Above"), (-1, "Below")):
    part = large[np.sign(large) == sign]
    idx = part.index.to_frame(index=False)
    latex.report(f"dataEzeroDiff{tag}", len(part),
                 desc=f"Curated life tables whose article e0 is {tag.lower()} the HMD's by more than 0.01 years")
    latex.report(f"dataEzeroDiff{tag}Pops", int(idx["pop"].nunique()) if len(part) else 0,
                 desc=f"Populations of those tables ({tag.lower()})")
    latex.report(f"dataEzeroDiff{tag}Max", float(part.abs().max()) if len(part) else 0.0, digits=3,
                 desc=f"Largest |difference| of those tables ({tag.lower()}, years)")
    latex.report(f"dataEzeroDiff{tag}LtenMax", float(l110.reindex(part.index).max()) if len(part) else 0.0,
                 digits=5, desc=f"Largest survival to exact age 110 in those tables ({tag.lower()})")
    latex.report(f"dataEzeroDiff{tag}LtenMin", float(l110.reindex(part.index).min()) if len(part) else 0.0,
                 digits=5, desc=f"Smallest survival to exact age 110 in those tables ({tag.lower()})")
    print(f"{tag}: {len(part)} tables, {idx['pop'].nunique()} populations "
          f"({', '.join(sorted(idx['pop'].unique()))}); sexes {idx['sex'].astype(str).value_counts().to_dict()}; "
          f"years {idx['year'].min()}–{idx['year'].max()}; |diff| ≤ {part.abs().max():.3f}; "
          f"l110 {l110.reindex(part.index).min():.2e}–{l110.reindex(part.index).max():.2e}")
```

## Where are the empty cells?

Of the $C \times T$ country-year cells, most are empty simply because a
population's series had not started.  The table accounts for every
unobserved cell.

```{python}
#| label: tbl-s3-missing-year-audit
#| code-summary: "Table: classification of the unobserved country-year cells"
excl = cur["exclusions"]
excl = excl[excl["pop"].isin(tensor["pops"])]
excluded_keys = set(zip(excl["pop"], excl["year"]))
cats = {"Outside the population's HMD series": 0,
        "Exceptional (masked for the disruption model)": 0,
        "Removed in curation": 0,
        "Gap inside the HMD series (not published)": 0}
for i, pop in enumerate(tensor["pops"]):
    hmd = lt_raw.loc[lt_raw["pop"] == pop, "year"]
    first, last = hmd.min(), hmd.max()
    for j, y in enumerate(years):
        if observed[i, j]:
            continue
        if exceptional[i, j]:
            cats["Exceptional (masked for the disruption model)"] += 1
        elif (pop, y) in excluded_keys:
            cats["Removed in curation"] += 1
        elif y < first or y > last:
            cats["Outside the population's HMD series"] += 1
        else:
            cats["Gap inside the HMD series (not published)"] += 1
audit = pd.DataFrame({"Category": list(cats), "Country-years": list(cats.values())})
audit["Share (\\%)"] = 100 * audit["Country-years"] / audit["Country-years"].sum()
total = int(audit["Country-years"].sum())
assert total == int((~observed).sum()), "every unobserved cell must be classified exactly once"
latex.save_table(audit, "tbl-s3-missing-year-audit",
                 f"Classification of the {latex.fmt_int(total)} unobserved country-year cells "
                 f"($O_{{c,t}} = 0$) of the tensor.",
                 formats={"Share (\\%)": "{:.1f}"}, column_format="lrr")
latex.report("dataNUnobserved", total, desc="Unobserved country-year cells")
```

## The change ledger

Every change made to the published HMD data on the way to the tensor is
listed in one file, `results/ledger/data-changes.csv`, with one row per
change: the step, the rule, the population, sex and years affected, and
the details.  It is generated from the records the pipeline keeps as it
works — the curation exclusions (chapter d3), the event labels (d4), the
pooling blocks (d5) and the floor applied here — so it is complete by
construction.

```{python}
#| label: d6-ledger
#| code-summary: "Assemble and write the change ledger"
ledger = []
for r in cur["exclusions"].itertuples(index=False):
    ledger.append({"step": "d3 curation", "rule": r.rule, "pop": r.pop, "sex": "both",
                   "first_year": r.year, "last_year": r.year, "detail": r.detail})
exc = labels[labels["label"] > 0]
for r in exc.itertuples(index=False):
    ledger.append({"step": "d4 exceptional", "rule": f"masked: {r.type}", "pop": r.pop, "sex": "both",
                   "first_year": r.year, "last_year": r.year,
                   "detail": f"event {r.event_id} (all matches: {r.all_events})"})
for r in pooled["blocks"].itertuples(index=False):
    ledger.append({"step": "d5 pooling", "rule": "valley pooling", "pop": r.pop, "sex": r.sex,
                   "first_year": r.first, "last_year": r.last,
```
